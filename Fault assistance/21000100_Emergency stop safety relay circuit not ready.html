Emergency stop safety relay circuit not ready
Effect on machine operation:
▪
Immediate Stop
The machine stops immediately
If a reference run is necessary after elimination of the fault and before a machine restart, this is shown on the touchscreen.
Cause
Elimination
The activated contacts do not match the external machine conditions.
    ▪
    Check the associated switching unit inside the control cabinet.
    ▪
     Check the contacts on emergency stop buttons.
The contact on the emergency stop button did not activate properly.
    ▪
    Push the emergency stop button once and then unlock it again. Then press the ACKNOWLEDGE button on the touchscreen to acknowledge the fault message.
The emergency stop button is defective.
    ▪
    Check the emergency stop button and replace it if necessary.
The emergency stop relay is defective.
    ▪
    Check the emergency stop relay and replace it if necessary.
