Evaluation unit is not ready for operation
Effect on machine operation:
    ▪
    Homerun stop
The machine ends the current movements and then remains in the home position. It may take several cycles for the current movements to end and the machine to stop, if the process requires it.
Information on the occurrence of faults when the machine is at a standstill can be found in the corresponding section in this chapter .
Cause
Elimination
The scanner (consisting of scan head or camera and the corresponding evaluation unit) is defective or adjusted incorrectly.
    ▪
    Check the function and adjustment of the scan head or the camera.
    ▪
    Check evaluation unit function and adjustment.
The power supply was interrupted.
    ▪
    Check the power supply.
Wire break in the cabling.
    ▪
    Check the cabling for the individual components.
